# Contributor guidance

- Use [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/) for every
  commit: `<type>[optional scope]: <description>`. Examples include
  `feat: add Bluetooth onboarding`, `fix: handle reconnects`, and
  `docs: explain credential recovery`.
- Keep `research/` and `tools/` local and untracked. Do not commit research artifacts,
  captures, decompiled apps, device credentials, or account passwords.
- `CLAUDE.md` is a symlink to this file; maintain the shared guidance here.
